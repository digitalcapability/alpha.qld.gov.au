Start here

Welcome to the Queensland Government Digital Capability Hub.

Digital capability is not about being an ICT specialist. It is about using digital technologies, information and data confidently, critically, safely and responsibly in everyday work.

There are different ways to begin. You can understand the framework, reflect on your capability, or explore practical learning that supports your work.

Three ways to begin

Learn about the five DigComp 3.0 competence areas and the 21 competences.

What would you like to learn more about?

Start with something you do, a question you have or an area you would like to improve. Each topic links to the relevant parts of the DigComp framework.

Finding and managing information

Learn how to find, assess, organise and use information effectively and responsibly.

  • Finding reliable information
  • Evaluating online sources and AI-generated information
  • Organising files, records and documents
  • Retrieving information when it is needed

Relevant competences

  • 1.1 Browsing, searching and filtering information
  • 1.2 Evaluating information
  • 1.3 Managing information
Explore Information Search, Evaluation and Management

Working and communicating with others online

Learn how to communicate, share, collaborate and participate appropriately in digital environments.

  • Choosing an appropriate communication channel
  • Sharing files and information
  • Collaborating across teams and agencies
  • Participating in digital communities
  • Managing digital identity and conduct

Relevant competences

  • 2.1 Interacting through and with digital technologies
  • 2.2 Sharing through digital technologies
  • 2.3 Engaging in citizenship through digital technologies
  • 2.4 Collaborating through digital technologies
  • 2.5 Digital conduct
  • 2.6 Managing digital identity
Explore Communication and Collaboration

Creating and improving digital content

Learn how to create, adapt, combine and use digital content ethically, responsibly and accessibly.

  • Writing documents and creating presentations
  • Creating accessible digital content
  • Revising and combining existing content
  • Using AI to support content creation
  • Using images, copyright material and licences appropriately
  • Understanding how automation and programming concepts affect work

Relevant competences

  • 3.1 Developing digital content
  • 3.2 Integrating and re-elaborating digital content
  • 3.3 Copyright and licences
  • 3.4 Computational thinking and programming
Explore Content Creation

Staying safe, well and responsible

Learn how to protect devices, information, privacy, wellbeing and the environment when using digital technologies.

  • Protecting accounts and devices
  • Handling personal and sensitive information
  • Using AI and other digital tools responsibly
  • Supporting digital wellbeing
  • Reducing the environmental impact of digital work

Relevant competences

  • 4.1 Protecting devices
  • 4.2 Protecting personal data and privacy
  • 4.3 Protecting health and wellbeing
  • 4.4 Protecting the environment
Explore Safety, Wellbeing and Responsible Use

Solving problems and improving work

Learn how to identify needs, solve problems, choose suitable digital responses and continue building capability.

  • Troubleshooting common digital issues
  • Identifying opportunities to improve a process
  • Selecting an appropriate digital tool
  • Using technology creatively
  • Developing your own capability and supporting others

Relevant competences

  • 5.1 Solving technical problems
  • 5.2 Identifying needs and technological responses
  • 5.3 Creatively using digital technologies
  • 5.4 Identifying and addressing digital competence gaps
Explore Problem Identification and Solving

Not sure where to begin?

Start with a workplace activity you would like to do more confidently or effectively. You do not need to work through the framework in order, and you do not need to explore every competence at once.

Learning and resources

Explore resources that support practical learning

In preparation for the PoC launch, the Queensland Government and Australian Government resources below have been reviewed and considered for alignment to the PoC self-assessment tool and Digital Capability Hub site. They support alignment, wherever possible, with current government frameworks, policies, standards, guidance and related capability initiatives.

Key frameworks and capability sources reviewed

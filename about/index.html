Purpose

The Digital Capability Hub helps public sector employees understand and apply the Queensland Government Digital Capability Framework, which is based on DigComp 3.0.

The Hub provides a practical way to explore the framework's capability areas, competences and proficiency levels, helping employees understand the knowledge, skills and attitudes needed to work confidently, critically, safely and responsibly in digital environments.

The Hub also supports capability reflection and development by connecting users to relevant learning resources, guidance materials, tools and policy sources that may help build capability in specific areas.

The Hub is not a curriculum, training program, certification pathway or comprehensive catalogue of digital learning. It does not prescribe mandatory learning activities or provide all of the information needed to develop digital capability.

Learning and development remain self-directed. The resources included throughout the Hub are intended as signposts to relevant learning opportunities, guidance and reference materials. Users are responsible for identifying their own development needs, selecting learning activities that are relevant to their role, and applying what they learn in their work.

The Hub does not replace agency-specific policies, procedures, legislative requirements, standards, approved technologies or official advice. Employees should continue to follow the requirements and guidance that apply within their agency.

Digital capability development is an ongoing process. The Hub supports that process by providing a shared framework for understanding capability and helping users discover resources that may support their continued growth.
